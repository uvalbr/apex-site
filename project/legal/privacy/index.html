Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Template notice. This page is a plain-language template provided for transparency. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed and finalized by qualified counsel before being relied upon.

Last updated: 2026

APEX Project ("APEX," "we," "us," or "our") is an independent contractor-matching service for Florida property owners. We are not a lender, a contractor, or a PACE program administrator. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our site, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to submit through our forms and matching tools, which typically includes:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number and email address)
  • Project details (the type of upgrade you are considering and your goals)
  • Property details (such as your property type and general location in Florida)

We do not ask you to submit sensitive financial account numbers through this site. We may also automatically collect basic technical data (such as your device type and general usage of the site) to keep the service working and secure.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Match you with vetted, licensed contractors suited to your project
  • Contact you about your project and coordinate next steps
  • Respond to your questions and improve our matching service

How we share your information

Because the core purpose of APEX is to connect you with someone who can do the work, we share the project and contact details you submit with the matched contractor(s) in our network so they can reach out about your project. We also share your details with our call center and service partners who help us follow up and coordinate your request.

We do not sell your personal information for unrelated purposes. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights, our users, or the public.

Calls and text messages (SMS) consent

By submitting your information through our forms, you agree that APEX and our contractor and call-center partners may call and text you at the phone number you provide about your project, including by means of an automatic telephone dialing system (autodialer) and prerecorded or artificial voice messages.

Consent to receive these calls or texts is not a condition of any purchase. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency may vary. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages, or reply HELP for assistance.

Your choices and data rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have submitted, and you may ask us to stop contacting you, subject to applicable law. Florida residents may have additional rights regarding their information. To make a request, contact us using the information on our site and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Who we are (and are not)

APEX is an independent matching service. We do not provide financing, perform construction work, or administer any PACE program. Financing options, including PACE, are offered and approved by third parties under their own terms, and the work is performed by independent licensed contractors. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of our site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
