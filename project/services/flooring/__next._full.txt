22:T46c,Florida is uniquely hard on floors. Year-round humidity, frequent rain, sandy soil, and the standing risk of flooding from storms and burst plumbing all punish moisture-sensitive materials. Solid hardwood and traditional laminate can cup, swell, buckle, or grow mold when humidity stays high or water finds its way under the surface — which is why so many Miami-Dade homes have moved to waterproof tile and luxury vinyl plank (LVP). These materials shrug off humidity, clean up easily after tracked-in sand and pool water, and survive a wet season that would ruin lesser flooring.

There's also a hurricane-and-flooding angle that's specific to South Florida. Homes that have taken on water — whether from a roof breach, storm surge, or a slab leak — frequently need full flooring rebuilds, and that work is rarely budgeted for in advance. Waterproof, hard-surface flooring is the smart rebuild choice because it gives you a floor that can handle the next event with far less damage.
