Why roofing

Florida's insurance crisis means aging roofs are a top cause of non-renewal; a new code-compliant roof is both a resilience and an insurability play, especially in the HVHZ counties.

  • Hurricane-rated roofing qualifies as a wind-resistance upgrade under Florida PACE
  • New roof can reduce insurance premiums and prevent non-renewal
  • Cool / reflective roofing options add energy savings
  • Licensed, insured Florida roofers — not out-of-state crews
  • $0 down, repaid on your tax bill over the roof's lifespan

Typical roofing projects

  • Full roof replacement (shingle, tile, or metal)
  • Storm-damaged roof rebuild
  • Reflective / cool-roof energy upgrade
  • Roof deck and secondary water barrier to code

Roofing in Miami

Built for Miami homes

Miami sits in a High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), so impact-rated products must carry a Miami-Dade County Notice of Acceptance (NOA) — the strictest wind-borne-debris standard in the country. Much of the housing stock predates modern wind codes, which makes hurricane-hardening both a safety upgrade and a path to lower windstorm insurance. With Miami's year-round cooling load, energy-efficient windows, roofing, and HVAC also cut high power bills — and qualifying upgrades are PACE-eligible here, financed at $0 down through the property-tax bill.

Miami-Dade requires NOA product approval for impact windows and doors; permits run through Miami-Dade RER.

Roofing in Miami — questions

Can I finance a whole new roof with no money down?+

Yes. Roof replacement is PACE-eligible in Florida as a wind-resistance improvement, so you can do the full job with $0 down and repay it over time on your property tax bill.

Will a new roof help with my insurance?+

Frequently. Insurers in Florida weigh roof age and wind rating heavily; a new code-compliant roof can improve insurability and may earn mitigation credits.

What if my roof was damaged in a storm?+

We can match you with a vetted contractor and lay out your financing. PACE can cover the rebuild even when insurance falls short of the full cost.

Do you serve Miami?+

Yes — we match Miami property owners with vetted, licensed and insured contractors for roofing, across Miami-Dade County. Qualifying projects can be financed through PACE at $0 down.
