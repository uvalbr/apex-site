Overview

Open, humidity-tolerant kitchen materials and ventilation matter in Florida's climate, and a modern kitchen is a top driver of resale value in the hot Miami-Dade market.

Your kitchen is the room your family actually lives in — where the morning coffee gets made, where homework happens, where everyone ends up at a party regardless of how much space the rest of the house has. In a Florida home, it's also the room that takes the most abuse from heat, humidity, and hard water, and the single space that most influences what a buyer thinks the moment they walk in. A kitchen remodel done well does two jobs at once: it makes daily life noticeably better, and it adds tangible resale value in one of the most competitive housing markets in the country.

APEX Project is an independent contractor-matching and financing service for Florida property owners, with a focus on Miami-Dade. We are not a contractor, a lender, or a design-build firm. What we do is connect you with a vetted, licensed Florida contractor who handles your project from design through final walkthrough, and arrange financing so the cost isn't a wall between you and the kitchen you want. For a kitchen remodel specifically, that financing is our $0-down consumer program — start with no money down, make no payments for the first 12–18 months, then move to low monthly payments. It is honest to say up front: this is a payment plan, not a grant and not free money. It's a way to do the project now and pay for it over time.

We want to be equally clear about one thing many homeowners ask: a kitchen remodel does not qualify for PACE financing. PACE in Florida is restricted by statute to energy efficiency, renewable energy, and hurricane/wind resistance — cosmetic and lifestyle improvements like cabinets, counters, and layout changes fall outside it. If your project happens to include a genuinely energy-efficient component (say, a high-efficiency appliance package or efficient replacement windows in the kitchen), that specific item could potentially be financed separately through PACE — but the kitchen itself goes through the consumer program. We'd rather tell you that on the way in than surprise you later.

Why it matters in Florida

Florida kitchens fight a battle that kitchens in drier states never face. Year-round humidity warps low-grade cabinetry, swells particleboard boxes, and feeds mold behind sinks and under dishwashers if the materials and ventilation aren't chosen for the climate. Salt air near the coast corrodes cheap hardware and appliance finishes. Hard water leaves scale on fixtures and shortens the life of faucets and disposals. A kitchen built with Florida in mind — moisture-resistant cabinet construction, sealed and heat-tolerant countertop surfaces like quartz or porcelain, proper range-hood ventilation that actually exhausts outside, and corrosion-rated hardware — simply lasts longer here than a builder-grade kitchen ever will. There's also the market reality of Miami-Dade. The housing stock skews older, and many homes still carry original or dated kitchens that quietly cap their value. In a market where buyers move fast and compare aggressively, an updated, open, well-lit kitchen is consistently one of the top features that drives offers and shortens time on market. A remodel here isn't just a comfort upgrade — in many Miami-Dade neighborhoods it's one of the highest-leverage improvements you can make to the property itself.

Financing

$0-down financing for your Kitchen Remodeling

Kitchen remodels are financed through APEX's $0-down consumer program, not through PACE. PACE (Florida statute s.163.08) is limited to energy-efficiency, renewable-energy, and wind/hurricane-resistance improvements that attach to and are repaid through your property-tax bill — a kitchen remodel is cosmetic and lifestyle work, so it does not qualify. The consumer program works differently: you start the project with $0 down, make no payments for the first 12–18 months, and then move to low monthly payments for the remainder of the term. It is important to be clear that this is a financing arrangement — a loan repaid over time — not a grant, a rebate, or free money. You will be responsible for repaying the financed amount on the schedule you agree to. Approval and exact terms depend on standard financing factors and the lending partner's criteria, and you'll always see the full terms in writing before committing. One useful note: if your kitchen project includes a specific component that genuinely qualifies as energy-efficient — for example a high-efficiency appliance package or efficient replacement windows in the kitchen wall — that individual item could potentially be routed to PACE financing separately, while the rest of the kitchen stays on the consumer program. That has to be verified item by item against PACE eligibility rules, and APEX will tell you honestly whether anything in your project fits.

FAQ

Kitchen Remodeling — questions

Can I use PACE financing for my kitchen remodel?+

No. Florida PACE law covers only energy-efficiency, renewable-energy, and hurricane-resistance improvements — a kitchen remodel is cosmetic and lifestyle work, so it isn't eligible. You can still do the kitchen with $0 down through our consumer financing program: no payments for 12–18 months, then low monthly payments. If a specific item in your project (like an efficient appliance package or efficient windows) genuinely qualifies, that one piece could potentially route to PACE separately.

Is the $0-down financing free money or a grant?+

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. It's a payment plan — a loan you repay over time. The benefit is that you start with nothing down and make no payments for the first 12–18 months, then low monthly payments after that. It's a way to do the project now and pay for it gradually, not a giveaway.

Who actually does the remodel — is APEX the contractor?+

No. APEX is an independent matching and financing service. The work is done by a vetted, licensed Florida contractor we connect you with, who handles the project from design through the final walkthrough. We're not the contractor and not the lender — we match you to the right people and arrange financing.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?+

It depends on scope. A cosmetic refresh can be a couple of weeks; a full gut with custom cabinetry and a layout change is typically several weeks to a couple of months, including time for cabinets and counters to be fabricated. Your matched contractor gives you a realistic timeline in writing before work starts.

What materials hold up best in Florida kitchens?+

For our climate, moisture-resistant cabinet construction matters more than it does up north, and heat- and stain-tolerant counters like quartz or porcelain tend to outperform cheaper surfaces. Proper range-hood ventilation that exhausts outdoors helps control humidity and cooking heat. Your contractor will steer you toward choices built for Florida rather than builder-grade.

Do I need permits for a kitchen remodel in Miami-Dade?+

Often yes — especially when plumbing, electrical, or structural changes (like moving a wall or adding an island) are involved. Your licensed contractor handles pulling the required permits as part of the job, which is one of the reasons working with a vetted, licensed pro matters here.

Can I just replace cabinets and counters without a full gut?+

Absolutely. A cabinet-and-countertop replacement or a targeted refresh is a very common project and a cost-effective way to modernize without reconfiguring the whole room. The contractor scopes it to what you actually want — you don't have to commit to a full remodel to make a real difference.

Will a remodeled kitchen really add resale value?+

In Miami-Dade, an updated, open, well-lit kitchen is consistently one of the strongest value-drivers, particularly given how much of the housing stock still has older, dated kitchens. We won't quote you a specific return figure — that depends on your home and market — but a modern kitchen is widely considered one of the highest-leverage improvements you can make.

What's the first step to get started?+

Tell us about your kitchen and your timeline. There's no upfront cost to get matched with a licensed contractor, and you'll get a written estimate before any work or financing is committed. From there, it's your decision whether to move forward.
