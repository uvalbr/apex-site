1a:T59e,Kitchen remodels are financed through APEX's $0-down consumer program, not through PACE. PACE (Florida statute s.163.08) is limited to energy-efficiency, renewable-energy, and wind/hurricane-resistance improvements that attach to and are repaid through your property-tax bill — a kitchen remodel is cosmetic and lifestyle work, so it does not qualify. The consumer program works differently: you start the project with $0 down, make no payments for the first 12–18 months, and then move to low monthly payments for the remainder of the term. It is important to be clear that this is a financing arrangement — a loan repaid over time — not a grant, a rebate, or free money. You will be responsible for repaying the financed amount on the schedule you agree to.

Approval and exact terms depend on standard financing factors and the lending partner's criteria, and you'll always see the full terms in writing before committing. One useful note: if your kitchen project includes a specific component that genuinely qualifies as energy-efficient — for example a high-efficiency appliance package or efficient replacement windows in the kitchen wall — that individual item could potentially be routed to PACE financing separately, while the rest of the kitchen stays on the consumer program.
