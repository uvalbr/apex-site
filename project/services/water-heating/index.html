Efficient and tankless water-heating upgrades that trim a quiet, constant line on your energy bill.

Why it matters in Florida

Water heating runs constantly in a Florida household; efficient or solar water heating quietly lowers the bill year-round in a climate that's ideal for solar-assisted systems.

  • Efficient and solar water heating are PACE-eligible energy improvements
  • Tankless and heat-pump units cut standby energy waste
  • Endless hot water with a smaller footprint
  • Pairs naturally with a broader energy package
  • $0 down, repaid on your property tax bill

Typical projects

  • Tankless water heater installation
  • Heat-pump (hybrid) water heater upgrade
  • Solar water-heating system
  • High-efficiency tank replacement
Financing: PACE-eligible as an energy-efficiency / renewable improvement under Florida statute s.163.08. High-efficiency, tankless, heat-pump, and solar water heaters qualify for $0-down PACE financing repaid on your tax bill. Qualifies only for high-efficiency / heat-pump units that meet the program's efficiency threshold.
