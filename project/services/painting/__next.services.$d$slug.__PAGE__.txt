1b:T521,Painting is financed through APEX's consumer financing program — not PACE. PACE (Florida statute s.163.08) is limited by law to energy-efficiency, renewable-energy, and wind/hurricane-resistance improvements repaid through your property-tax bill, and cosmetic painting does not qualify. So instead, the consumer program offers $0 down, no payments for the first 12 to 18 months, and low monthly payments after that introductory period. It is a loan, not a grant and not free money — you repay the full financed amount over the term, and the deferred-payment window simply moves the start of payments later, it does not reduce what you owe.

Approval is based on standard consumer-financing criteria rather than your property-tax status, and APEX is the matchmaker — we help you connect with the program and understand the terms, but we are not the lender. You will see the rate, term, and monthly amount in writing and can decline at any point before signing. One honest caveat: if your exterior project includes a genuinely reflective or insulating "cool" coating with a measurable energy benefit, a narrow energy angle could in rare cases touch a PACE-eligible product — but do not assume it; that has to be verified case by case, and standard decorative painting is firmly in the consumer-program
