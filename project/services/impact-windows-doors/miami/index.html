Why impact windows & doors

In a hurricane state, impact-rated openings are the single highest-impact resilience upgrade — they often trigger windstorm insurance discounts and meet Miami-Dade / Broward HVHZ (High-Velocity Hurricane Zone) code.

  • Miami-Dade NOA hurricane protection — no plywood, no shutters
  • Wind-resistance upgrades are squarely PACE-eligible under Florida law
  • Can lower homeowners + windstorm insurance premiums
  • ENERGY STAR impact glass reduces cooling costs year-round
  • $0 down — repaid on your property tax bill over time

Impact Windows & Doors in Miami

Built for Miami homes

Miami sits in a High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), so impact-rated products must carry a Miami-Dade County Notice of Acceptance (NOA) — the strictest wind-borne-debris standard in the country. Much of the housing stock predates modern wind codes, which makes hurricane-hardening both a safety upgrade and a path to lower windstorm insurance. With Miami's year-round cooling load, energy-efficient windows, roofing, and HVAC also cut high power bills — and qualifying upgrades are PACE-eligible here, financed at $0 down through the property-tax bill.

Miami-Dade requires NOA product approval for impact windows and doors; permits run through Miami-Dade RER.

FAQ

Impact Windows & Doors in Miami — questions

Do impact windows really qualify for PACE?+

Yes. Florida PACE programs explicitly cover wind-resistance and hurricane-hardening improvements, and impact-rated windows and doors are one of the most common PACE projects in Miami-Dade. You can do the whole job with $0 down.

Will impact windows lower my insurance?+

Often, yes. Many Florida insurers offer windstorm-mitigation credits for code-approved impact openings. We can match you with a contractor who provides the documentation your insurer needs.

How is the financing repaid?+

Through PACE, repaid as an assessment on your property tax bill over several years — no upfront cash, and the balance can transfer to the next owner if you sell.

Do you serve Miami?+

Yes — we match Miami property owners with vetted, licensed and insured contractors for impact windows & doors, across Miami-Dade County. Qualifying projects can be financed through PACE at $0 down.
