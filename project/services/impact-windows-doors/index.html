Hurricane-rated impact windows and doors that protect your home, cut your insurance, and lower your energy bills.

Why it matters in Florida

In a hurricane state, impact-rated openings are the single highest-impact resilience upgrade — they often trigger windstorm insurance discounts and meet Miami-Dade / Broward HVHZ (High-Velocity Hurricane Zone) code.

  • Miami-Dade NOA hurricane protection — no plywood, no shutters
  • Wind-resistance upgrades are squarely PACE-eligible under Florida law
  • Can lower homeowners + windstorm insurance premiums
  • ENERGY STAR impact glass reduces cooling costs year-round
  • $0 down — repaid on your property tax bill over time

Typical projects

  • Whole-home impact window replacement
  • Impact-rated front and sliding glass doors
  • Garage door wind-rating upgrade
  • Single-room or hurricane-zone-facing window swap
Financing: Fully PACE-eligible. Impact (wind-resistant) windows and doors qualify under Florida PACE statute (s.163.08) as hurricane-hardening improvements. $0 down, repaid as a line item on your annual property tax bill — and the balance can transfer with the home if you sell.
