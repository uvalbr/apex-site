Why partner with APEX

Spend your time on jobs, not chasing leads

APEX is an independent matching service. We are not a lender, a contractor, or a PACE administrator — our job is to bring the right homeowner and the right contractor together, with financing already on the table.

Qualified, financing-ready leads

We connect you with property owners who've already told us about their project and their interest in $0-down financing — not cold names from a list.

Matched to your trade & area

You hear about projects that fit what you actually do and where you actually work. No wasted drives across three counties for jobs outside your scope.

$0-down project paths

Many homeowners we work with use $0-down financing for eligible energy and resilience upgrades — PACE is one option. We help line up the paperwork so projects move forward cleanly.

You only pay for what converts

Our model is built around real, closed work — not paying upfront for clicks or impressions that never turn into a signed job.

What we look for

Who's a good fit for the network

We keep the network small and qualified so homeowners trust the match — and so the contractors in it get a steady stream of well-scoped work. To be considered, you should be:

  • Properly licensed for the work you perform in Florida
  • Active general liability insurance (and workers' comp where required)
  • Experience with PACE-eligible work — energy, water, wind and resilience upgrades
  • Committed to honest scoping, fair pricing and clean communication with homeowners

How to apply

Tell us about your company — your trades, license and insurance details, the counties you serve, and the kind of projects you want more of. We review every application and reach out to contractors who fit current homeowner demand.

Honest note: being in the network doesn't guarantee a volume of leads. Matches depend on real homeowner demand in your trade and service area.
