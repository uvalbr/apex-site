Upgrade your home with $0 down through PACE

PACE (Property Assessed Clean Energy) lets eligible Florida property owners finance qualifying improvements with no money down and repay over time as a line item on the annual property tax bill — approval is based mainly on home equity, not a credit score. Financing

What is PACE?

PACE (Property Assessed Clean Energy) lets you finance qualifying property upgrades with no money down — repaid as a line on your property tax bill.

PACE is our specialty — but not our only option.

We also work with other Miami-area programs, rebates, and financing paths. We'll point you to whatever fits your project best.

Important disclosure

How APEX fits in

APEX Project is an independent contractor-matching service for Florida property owners. We are not a lender, not a contractor, and not a PACE administrator. PACE and any other financing are provided by third-party programs and approved providers, and are always subject to eligibility, underwriting, program terms, and applicable Florida law.

PACE is repaid as an assessment on your property tax bill — it is financing, not a grant or rebate, and it is not free. Program availability, terms, and qualifying improvements vary by county and can change. Nothing here is financial, tax, or legal advice. Review all terms with the financing provider, and consult your own advisor, before signing.
