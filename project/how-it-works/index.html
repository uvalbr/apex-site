The process

From idea to done — on your side the whole way

We sit between you and the contractors, never the other way around. Free for property owners, with no obligation at any step.

01

Tell us about your project

Share your project type, budget range, and where your property is. It takes about a minute — no documents, no credit check, no commitment.

  • Pick from common Florida upgrades
  • Tell us your county and property type
  • No credit pull to get started
02

We match you with the right pro

We screen our network for the licensed, insured contractor who actually fits your job, your area, and your timeline — and lay your PACE financing options on the table.

  • Matched by trade, budget & location
  • Only vetted, PACE-approved contractors
  • Clear $0-down financing, explained
03

Get the work done — $0 down

Your contractor does the work with nothing out of pocket. For PACE-eligible upgrades, you repay over time as a line on your property-tax bill.

  • Start with $0 down
  • No payments for 12–18 months
  • Repaid on your tax bill (PACE-eligible)
