Who we are

One service, working for you

“When the match is right and the financing is honest, every side wins.”

APEX exists to solve a simple problem for Florida property owners: choosing the right contractor for a major home upgrade is hard, and paying for it up front is harder. We sit between you and the work — an independent layer that matches your project, budget, and location to the best vetted, licensed contractor in your area, and arranges financing so qualifying upgrades can be done with $0 down through PACE.

We are not a construction company. APEX does not pour concrete, install windows, or put on a roof. We are not a lender and not a PACE administrator either. What we do is listen to what you need, screen the professionals who could deliver it, and connect you with the one that fits — then help you understand your financing options so the decision is yours to make with clear eyes.

Because we don't do the work ourselves, our network of contractors isn't our competition — it's our strength. We complement the licensed professionals we partner with by sending them well-matched, ready-to-move owners, and we hold them to the standard you'd want. That alignment is the whole point: when the match is right and the financing is honest, every side wins.
